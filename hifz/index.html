Hifz: Quran Memorization Checker – Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026. App package: app.hifz.quran

The short version

Hifz does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Your recitation is checked entirely on your phone. Your voice never leaves your device.

Microphone and your recitation

The app asks for microphone access only so it can listen while you recite. The audio is processed in the phone's memory by an on-device speech model to check your words against the Quran text, and is discarded right after the check. Recordings are never saved to storage, never uploaded and never shared.

Data we collect

None. Hifz has no account system, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising and no servers. We cannot see your recitation, your progress, your device identifiers or your usage.

Network use

None. The app does not have the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Everything, including the Quran text and the speech model, is included in the app download.

Permissions

Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) only, used as described above. You can turn it off at any time in Android settings; the Mushaf reader keeps working without it.

Children

Children often memorise the Quran, and the app can be used by them with a parent or teacher. Because the app collects no data from anyone, no personal information of children is collected.

Sources

Quran text: Tanzil Project (tanzil.net), used verbatim. Speech model: Tarteel AI whisper-tiny-ar-quran (Apache 2.0), fine-tuned on-device-ready by us. Runtime: ONNX Runtime (MIT).
