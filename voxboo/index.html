Voxboo: Voice Changer – Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026. App package: app.voxboo.voicechanger

The short version

Voxboo does not collect, store on any server, or transmit any personal data. Your voice is recorded and changed entirely on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

Microphone and your recordings

The app asks for microphone access only when you tap record or start Live mode. Recordings are processed on your phone. A recording stays on your device in the app's private storage. When you tap Save, a copy is also placed in your phone's Music/Voxboo folder so your music player can play it; you can switch this off in Settings. Live mode plays your changed voice straight back to your speaker or headphones and keeps nothing unless you tap "Record what you hear".

Files you import or type

If you import an audio or video file, the app reads only the file you pick, through Android's file picker. If you type text, it is turned into speech by your phone's own text-to-speech engine (for example Google Speech Services), which is covered by that engine's own privacy terms; Voxboo does not send the text anywhere itself.

Data we collect

None. Voxboo has no account system, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, no advertising ID and no servers. We cannot see your recordings, your files, your device identifiers or how you use the app.

Network use

The app does not have the Internet permission. The optional one-time "Voxboo Pro" purchase and the optional rating prompt are handled by the Google Play Store app on your phone, under Google's privacy policy. We never receive your name, email or payment details; Google only confirms to the app that Pro was bought.

Sharing

A recording leaves your phone only when you choose to share it (for example to WhatsApp) through Android's share sheet, or set it as a ringtone.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): to record your voice and for Live mode.
  • Modify system settings (WRITE_SETTINGS): only if you choose "Set as ringtone / notification / alarm"; Android asks you separately and the app uses it for nothing else.
  • Google Play billing: for the optional one-time Pro unlock.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include your saved Voxboo recordings and settings in that backup so they return when you change phones. This is controlled by you in Android settings and handled by Google; we have no access to it.

Deleting your data

Delete a recording in the app's Library (this also removes its copy in Music/Voxboo), or uninstall the app to remove everything stored in the app's private storage.

Children

Voxboo is a general-audience entertainment app. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no personal information from children.

Changes
