Gleamo Privacy Policy

App: Phone Storage Cleaner - Gleamo (app.gleamo.cleaner) · Last updated: 22 October 2026

The short version

Gleamo does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your photos, videos or any other information off your phone.

What the app accesses, and why

With your permission, Gleamo reads the photos and videos on your device (Android "Photos and videos" permission) to find duplicates, similar photos, screenshots, large videos and WhatsApp media. All of this analysis happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Gleamo deletes files only after you select them and confirm in Android's own system dialog.

On-device AI: to find blurry photos, WhatsApp greeting forwards and the best shot among similar photos, Gleamo analyses images on your device using Google ML Kit (text recognition and face detection). The AI models are provided by Google Play services on your phone, which may download them once; your photos are processed on the device and are never sent to Gleamo, Google or anyone else. Face detection only measures whether eyes are open and whether people smile; it does not identify who anyone is.

Video compressor: when you compress a video, Gleamo saves the smaller copy to your gallery (Movies/Gleamo) on your device. The original is removed only if you confirm it.

Duplicate contacts: with your permission (Android "Contacts" permission), Gleamo reads your contacts on the device to find contacts saved more than once, and merges them using Android's built-in contact linking. Your contacts are never uploaded, copied or shared.

Secret Space: photos and videos you move into Secret Space are stored in Gleamo's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Opening it requires your phone's own screen lock (PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint); Gleamo never sees or stores your lock or fingerprint. Uninstalling Gleamo deletes Secret Space files, so restore anything you want to keep first.

Data we collect

None. Gleamo has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Gleamo stores two small settings locally: your chosen language and the date it last showed the Google Play rating prompt. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Google Play

After a successful clean, Gleamo may show Google Play's built-in rating sheet. That sheet is provided by the Google Play Store app, not by Gleamo, and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. Google Play may also share anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as installs and crash counts) with us through the Play Console, as it does for every app.

Children

Gleamo does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
