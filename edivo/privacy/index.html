Edivo Privacy Policy

App: Edivo – Video Editor & Maker (Android package app.reelix.editor)
Developer: Codegram Studio
Effective: 27 November 2026

Short version. Edivo edits video on your phone. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and nothing to buy. Your photos, videos and projects never leave your phone. The app goes online only when you ask it to: the Stock search sends the words you type to Pixabay, and the Store, caption translation and the optional AI packs download files and send nothing about you.

What stays on your phone

  • Your projects, photos, videos, music, voice recordings and captions.
  • Everything the AI tools do: captions, translation, background removal, enhance, voice tools and the stabiliser all run on the device.
  • Mix: your profile, the posts you publish, your likes, comments, follows, reports and inbox are kept on your phone only. Nothing in Mix is sent to us or to anyone else, and Profile > Clear Mix removes it all.
  • Backups and exports are saved only where you choose to save them.

Stock search: the one feature that sends something you type

In the editor, Add > Stock lets you search free videos and photos from Pixabay.

What is sentTo whomWhy
The words you search for, the page number and your phone's language settingPixabay (pixabay.com), over an encrypted connectionTo show you matching videos and photos
A request for the file you pickedPixabayTo download that video or photo to your phone

We do not keep your searches. Search results are stored on your phone for up to 24 hours so the same search does not need the network again. Like any website, Pixabay sees the internet address the request comes from; its own policy is at pixabay.com/service/privacy.

Downloads that send nothing about you

  • Store. When you open the Store, or tap "Check for new", the app may download a list of extra templates, sounds, overlays and voices from a web folder we publish, and later the item you pick. Most Store items come with the app and need no internet.
  • Caption translation. Captions are translated on the phone. The first time you use a language, its translation files (about 30 MB) are downloaded from Google (Google ML Kit); your captions are not sent.
  • AI tool files and optional AI packs. Some AI tools (auto-captions, voice and music separation, smooth slow motion, smart suggestions, Indian-language typing) get their files from Google Play the first time you use them, and the optional AI packs when you ask for them, under Google's privacy policy. Afterwards they work offline.

Nothing about you or your projects is sent with these downloads; like any website, the host sees the internet address the request comes from. If you use none of these and never open Stock, the app does not go online.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account.
  • No location, contacts or device identifiers. The advertising ID permission is removed from the app.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services.
  • No payments: there is nothing to buy in Edivo.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the Stock search and the downloads described above.
  • Microphone: only while you record a voice-over, a voice sample or a screen recording with sound, and only when you start it.
  • Photos and videos: asked only if you tap Allow under Add > Media > Phone folders, so the app can list the folders on your phone. They are read on the phone to show and edit them; nothing is sent anywhere. Without it you can still pick files with Android's own picker.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi; called Location on Android 10 to 12): only when you send a project to, or receive one from, a nearby phone. The project goes straight from phone to phone through Google Play services' Nearby Connections; Edivo reads no location and nothing goes through a server.
  • Notifications: to show progress while an export, an AI job, a transfer or a screen recording runs, and, if you turn it on, Mix notes. Asked only when needed.
  • Screen recording: only if you start a screen recording.

Children

Edivo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything from your phone, use Settings > Reset app in Edivo, or uninstall the app.

Changes

If a future version changes what leaves your phone, this page and the in-app Privacy page will be updated before that version is released.
