Unblira: AI Photo Enhancer – Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026. App package: app.unblira.photo

The short version

Unblira does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. All photo processing happens on your device. Your photos never leave your phone.

Photos

You choose photos through the Android photo picker, which gives the app access only to the photos you select. Processing runs entirely on the device using on-device AI models. Results are written to your gallery (Pictures/Unblira) only when you tap Save. Sharing creates a temporary copy in the app's private cache that Android hands to the app you choose; it is deleted automatically.

Data we collect

None. Unblira has no account system, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK and no servers of its own. We do not see your photos, your device identifiers or your usage.

Network use

The app itself makes no network requests. The AI model files are delivered by Google Play (Play Asset Delivery) the first time you use each tool, under Google Play's own terms and privacy policy. After that, every tool works offline.

Permissions

Unblira requests no runtime permissions. It uses the system photo picker for input and the Android MediaStore for saving, neither of which requires storage permission.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Open-source components

Unblira uses open-source AI models and libraries, including GFPGAN (Apache 2.0), Real-ESRGAN (BSD-3), ClearRealityV1 (Apache 2.0), DDColor (Apache 2.0), Microsoft "Bringing Old Photos Back to Life" (MIT), fast neural style models from the ONNX model zoo (BSD-3), ONNX Runtime (MIT) and ML Kit face detection (Google Play services). Licence details are listed inside the app under About.
